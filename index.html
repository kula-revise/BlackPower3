The essay so far

Three moves down. The argument is now doing something difficult: conceding the strongest evidence against itself and still holding.

Black Power grew out of conditions that the Civil Rights Movement's legal victories never reached, so its militancy began as an answer to continued poverty and police brutality. At its core Black Power was a philosophy of self-definition and self-reliance, so its militancy has to be judged as one expression of that philosophy and not as its purpose. Malcolm X gave the militant strand its clearest voice by defending armed self-defence, yet the same argument insisted on self-respect and self-help.

An examiner reading that third sentence sees a candidate who is not hiding from the evidence. That is worth more than a page of description.
