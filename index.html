Where we are

In Lesson 1 you argued that Black Power grew out of conditions the law had never reached. That explains the anger. It does not yet explain the movement. This lesson does the hardest and most valuable thing in the whole topic: it says what the Black Power philosophy actually was. Get this right and the essay question in Lesson 5 becomes answerable, because that question asks you to judge the militancy in the context of the philosophy.

The essay so far

Two moves down. Read them together and you can already hear an argument forming.

Black Power grew out of conditions that the Civil Rights Movement's legal victories never reached, so its militancy began as an answer to continued poverty and police brutality. At its core Black Power was a philosophy of self-definition and self-reliance, so its militancy has to be judged as one expression of that philosophy and not as its purpose.

The second sentence is the one that makes the essay question answerable. Without it you are stuck listing militant acts, which answers a question you were not asked.
